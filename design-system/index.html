3. 4dp Grid System & Material 3 Elevation

All layouts, touch targets, and paddings align strictly to an 8-point / 4-point spacing grid.

Spacing Scale (4dp Grid)

• 4dp: Chip padding, inner icon gap
• 8dp: Button vertical padding, small gaps
• 12dp: Form control padding, action button margins
• 16dp: Screen margins, card paddings (Android standard)
• 24dp: Section separations, D-pad outer margins
• 32dp: Major screen transitions, Hero spacing

Elevation Scale (MD3 Shadows)

• Level 0: Flat containers (0dp)
• Level 1: Device Cards (2dp): 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.3)
• Level 2: D-Pad & FAB (6dp): 0 6px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.4)
• Level 3: Modals & Floating Overlays (12dp): 0 12px 32px rgba(0,0,0,0.6)

7. Accessibility (A11y) & Standards

✅ Minimum Touch Target: All interactive buttons >= 48dp x 48dp (WCAG 2.5.5 Level AAA compliance).
✅ Contrast Ratio: Text Primary (#F1F5F9) on Background (#0F172A) is 15.3:1 (Exceeds WCAG AAA requirement of 7:1).
✅ Indian Localization: Full bilingual English & Hindi interface with transliterated voice command processing.
✅ Haptic & Sound Feedback: Tactile 12ms audio synthesizer and vibration pulse on every button press.
✅ High Contrast & Large Text: Dedicated 1-tap toggles for Elder Mode and high-contrast OLED black theme.
